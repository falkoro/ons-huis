# Ons Huis

Interactive 3D walkthrough of our house, built with [three.js](https://threejs.org/) to plan a renovation, starting with the floors.

- Walk through the house, stairs included, or switch to the dollhouse overview per floor.
- Change wall colours, floor types and rugs per room, or apply a style preset.
- Doors open and close with their handles (click, tap or E).
- Logan and Gemma, our two dalmatians, live here too. Click one to pet it.
