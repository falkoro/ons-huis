# Ons Huis

Interactive 3D walkthrough of our house, built with [three.js](https://threejs.org/) to plan a renovation, starting with the floors.

- Walk mode (WASD + mouse, or the joystick on a phone) and a dollhouse overview per floor.
- Change wall colours, floor types and rugs per room, or apply a style preset.
- Doors open and close with their handles (click or press E).
- Logan and Gemma, our two dalmatians, live in the living room. Click one to pet it.
